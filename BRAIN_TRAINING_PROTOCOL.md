# DotRush Brain-Training Protocol

DotRush is a short-form attention and control trainer. It is not a medical product and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent cognitive conditions.

## Daily Use

Recommended session length: 3 to 8 minutes.

1. Start with **Daily Neuroset** or **Focus Sprint**.
2. Run one **Training Load** puzzle.
3. Read the post-run recommendation.
4. Do one follow-up rep based on the recommendation:
   - **Increase load**: move up one difficulty or try Speed Run.
   - **Hold**: repeat the same mode and reduce mistakes.
   - **Recover**: drop one load and rebuild clean control.
5. End with **Precision Lab** if errors were high.

## What To Watch

- **Accuracy**: wrong taps and wrong color choices.
- **Control**: whether the player finishes without failure or panic taps.
- **Pace**: efficient completion without turning the session into reckless speed.
- **Composite**: a simple 0-100 summary used for trend tracking.

## Weekly Balance

Across a week, touch all six systems at least once:

- Focus Sprint: sustained attention and sequencing
- Pressure Control: inhibition under time pressure
- Daily Neuroset: habit and recall
- Adaptive Flow: endurance and task switching
- Precision Lab: impulse control
- Processing Speed: rapid scanning

The profile screen highlights undertrained systems and the weakest average score so the next rep has a concrete purpose.

## Safety And Limits

- Keep sessions short; fatigue produces noisy scores.
- Stop if the game creates eye strain, frustration, or discomfort.
- Treat scores as practice feedback, not clinical measurement.
- The most useful signal is the trend over time, not one isolated score.
